# Copy voice — reference

When writing copy for this project (tour prompts, glossary definitions, explainer panels, error messages, button labels), match this voice.

## The rule

Dry, specific, confident without being smug. Technical users shouldn't feel patronized. Non-technical users shouldn't feel excluded. No one should feel marketed to.

## Banned words & phrases

These instantly land the copy in SaaS-landing-page territory. Rewrite around them.

- unlock, empower, supercharge, revolutionize, redefine, reimagine
- seamless, frictionless, effortless, magical, beautiful (as an adjective about the product itself)
- next-gen, cutting-edge, game-changing, disruptive
- "your journey", "the future of", "built for the way you…"
- exclamation points (unless in a direct quote from a user)
- "we" meaning the abstract company — this isn't a company
- "simply" — if it were simple you wouldn't need to say so

## Voice examples

### 🟢 Good

- **Button:** "Stake"
- **Explainer after stake:** "You just moved 100 mDAI from your wallet into the farm contract. It starts earning DAPP rewards immediately, at a rate that depends on how much everyone else has staked. Pull it back any time."
- **Glossary · liquidation:** "When a borrower misses their deadline, the lender can take the ETH they posted as collateral. The loan ends — lender keeps the ETH, borrower keeps whatever they borrowed."
- **Tour prompt:** "This number grows every second while anything is staked. The rate drops as more people join."
- **Error:** "Your wallet rejected the transaction. Nothing was charged."

### 🔴 Bad

- **Button:** "Stake Now 🚀"
- **Explainer:** "Congratulations! You've successfully unlocked your first staking reward! Your tokens are now earning yield in our decentralized farming protocol. Keep staking to maximize your earnings!"
- **Glossary · liquidation:** "Liquidation is a powerful risk-management mechanism in DeFi that allows lenders to protect their capital in the event of borrower default."
- **Tour prompt:** "🎉 Welcome! Let's walk you through this amazing DeFi experience together!"
- **Error:** "Something went wrong. Please try again."

## Length

Short. Tour prompts max 2 sentences. Glossary definitions max 3. Explainer panels max 4. Every word should survive a "would I delete this without losing meaning" test.

## Numbers

Mono font, tabular. When the number is the subject of the sentence, lead with it:

- 🟢 "100 mDAI moves into the farm."
- 🔴 "The amount of 100 mDAI will move into the farm."

## Tense + person

- Explainers about what just happened: past tense, second person.  ("You just moved 100 mDAI…")
- Tour prompts about what's next: present tense, second person. ("This number grows every second…")
- Glossary: definitional, no pronouns. ("Staking is the act of…")

## When in doubt

Read it in the voice of a financial journalist explaining a derivatives position to a colleague at lunch. Calm, technical, unhurried, assumes intelligence but not specific knowledge. That's the voice.
